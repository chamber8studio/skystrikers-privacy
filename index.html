Privacy Policy for SkyStrikers

Last Updated: October 2026

Application: SkyStrikers (Meta Horizon Store)

Developer / Data Controller: Dr. Panda Ltd. (Hong Kong)

Publisher: Chamber8 Studio

Privacy & Data Deletion Contact Email: developer@chamber-8.com

Dr. Panda Ltd. and Chamber8 Studio ("we", "our", or "us") are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable global data protection laws. This Privacy Policy explains what specific data is collected and processed when you use our VR game SkyStrikers ("the App") on the Meta Horizon platform, how that data is used and shared, and how you can request the deletion of your data.

1. Specific Data Collected Through Meta Horizon Platform Features

When you access and play SkyStrikers on the Meta Horizon platform, our App requests and processes specific user data through Meta Horizon Platform Features:

  • Meta User ID (USER_ID): A unique platform identifier provided by Meta Horizon.
  • Meta User Profile (USER_PROFILE): Your Meta Horizon profile information, such as your display name/username (alias) and avatar.
  • User Age Group: Age-group classification data provided by the Meta Horizon platform (where applicable) to ensure age-appropriate features and compliance.
  • In-App Purchases and/or Downloadable Content (IAP / DLC): Purchase receipts, entitlements, and transaction status records related to virtual items or downloadable content purchased within SkyStrikers.
  • Gameplay & Technical Data: Device hardware specifications, OS/Unity version, IP-derived country (IP addresses are not permanently stored by us), gameplay statistics, game progression, and session logs.

2. How We Use Collected Data

We strictly use the collected data only for the following purposes to operate and improve SkyStrikers:

  • Account Authentication & Cloud Saves (USER_ID): To authenticate your player account, bind your Meta User ID to our backend game service, and save/restore your game progression, settings, and unlocked content.
  • Multiplayer, Leaderboards & Social Features (USER_ID & USER_PROFILE): To display your username and avatar in multiplayer matches, player invitations, and leaderboards.
  • In-App Purchases & Entitlement Validation (USER_ID & IAP/DLC): To verify purchase transactions and deliver purchased virtual items or downloadable content to your account.
  • Age-Appropriate Experience (User Age Group): To comply with platform rules and apply age-appropriate settings or feature restrictions.
  • Analytics, Bug Fixing & Service Improvement: To diagnose crashes, maintain network/information security, prevent fraud/cheating, and understand gameplay usage to improve the App.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties or use your Meta Horizon user data for third-party advertising.

3. Third-Party Service Providers (Data Processors)

To operate SkyStrikers reliably, we use the following third-party service providers who process data strictly on our behalf:

  • Microsoft Azure PlayFab: Used as our backend game server to manage player account authentication (linking Meta User ID), cloud game saves/progression, leaderboards, multiplayer services, and in-app purchase validation. Privacy Policy: https://privacy.microsoft.com/
  • Mixpanel: Used for gameplay usage analytics to help us understand feature usage and improve SkyStrikers. Privacy Policy: https://mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies): Used for game engine diagnostics, performance monitoring, and aggregated usage analytics. Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide SkyStrikers' services, maintain your game account and entitlements, ensure security and anti-cheat integrity, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. How to Request Deletion of Your Data (User Data Deletion Mechanism)

All users of SkyStrikers have the right to request the deletion of their personal data and associated Meta Horizon platform data (including your User ID linkage, User Profile cache, PlayFab player account, and game progression) at any time.

To request data deletion:

  1. Send an email to: developer@chamber-8.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request - SkyStrikers
  3. Include your Meta Horizon Username and/or Meta User ID so we can locate and verify your account.

Once we verify your request, we will permanently delete your associated player data from our active servers and PlayFab backend within 30 days (except where limited retention is strictly required by law, such as statutory financial transaction records).

6. Additional Rights (EU / UK / GDPR & Other Regions)

Where applicable under local laws (such as GDPR in the EU/UK), you also have the right to access, rectify, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or request portability of your personal data, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, please contact developer@chamber-8.com.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization or platform compliance. If you believe we have unintentionally collected personal data from a child in violation of applicable laws, please contact us at developer@chamber-8.com so we can promptly delete such data.
